NAOMI — 10-layer QML sprite prototype
Source: standing black dress pixel-art Naomi.
All PNG are RGBA, 305x1024 pixels, share the identical origin.
Render in order: legs > body > hair_back > clothing > head > arm_right > arm_left > hair_front > eyes > mouth

Preview: assembly_preview.jpg and layers_overview.jpg.
The 10 layers form a recognisable character and are intended for gentle motion only.
Head/hand touch; moving head independently by many degrees needs more art.
Eyes and mouth each depict a single static expression; add closed-eye/talking variants for animation.
The source artwork did not contain hidden geometry; modest seam extensions are approximation.
Do not use heavy transformations and do not consider this Live2D-ready.
